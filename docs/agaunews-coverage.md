# Agaunews maker coverage

Checked 20 November 2026 against the [Agaunews sponsor guide](https://agaunews.com/where-to-buy-our-sponsors/). The guide has 50 named makers/issuers/distributors, plus three generic collecting categories, which are not mints.

Every named entry maps below to an automatic source or a dedicated dealer collection. **Direct** means a public catalogue or shop connected to that organization; it does not promise every product, live retail stock, or full historical coverage. **Dealer collection** means the named maker category at a separately attributed seller. Current health and scan limits appear in Raven Eye’s Sources & coverage.
